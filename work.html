My MSc Astrophysics project dissertation, which looks at trying to identify a stable mass transfer process in WD+FGK-type binaries, by contrasting the white dwarf mass against the binary instantaneous periastron period, and attempting to identify any correlation between the two and the rotational period of the main-sequence companion obtained via the Lomb-Scargle and Autocorrelation Function methods.
