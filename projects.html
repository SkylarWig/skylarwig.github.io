I am currently completing my masters degree in Astrophysics at the University of Sheffield, which involves a year long project.
This project is supervised by Dr. Steven Parsons, in which I am taking a list of identified White Dwarf binary systems, completing vigorous data analysis in order to determine a rotation period of these White Dwarfs companions, in attempt to see if there is a correlation between the rotation period of the White Dwarf and the binary it is in's mass transfer stability. This was completed using the Lomb-Scargle Periodogram and Autocorrelation Function methods of obtaining a rotational period, and then mapped on a colour map, against the White Dwarf mass and instantaneous periastron period, using a stable mass transfer function window taken from Garbutt et al. 2024.
