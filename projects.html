I am currently completing my masters degree in Astrophysics at the University of Sheffield, which involves a year long project.
This project is supervised by Dr. Steven Parsons, in which I am taking a list of identified White Dwarf binary systems, completing vigorous data analysis in order to determine a rotation period of these White Dwarfs, in attempt to see if there is a correlation between the rotation period of the White Dwarf and the binary it is in's mass transfer stability.
